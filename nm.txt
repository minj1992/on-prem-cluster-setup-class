Dashboard widgets

Section 1: Overview (is RabbitMQ healthy?)

Pods ready: how many of the 3 RabbitMQ pods are running.
Nodes sending metrics: how many pods are actually sending data.
Active alarms: must be 0. Anything else means RabbitMQ has stopped taking messages.
Pod restarts: how many times pods restarted. Should be 0.
Messages waiting: messages in queues not yet picked up.
Messages in progress: messages picked up but not finished yet.
Consumers: how many apps are reading messages.
Connections: how many apps are connected.

Section 2: Pods

Pod status table: one row per pod showing ready, metrics, restarts, alarms, memory and server.
Pod ready over time: green/red timeline of when pods were up or down.
CPU used: CPU per pod against its limit.
Memory used: memory per pod against its limit.

Section 3: Messages (is work flowing?)

Messages waiting and in progress: the backlog over time.
Messages in vs out: messages coming in vs going out per second.
Consumers, connections and queues: these counts over time.
Lost messages: messages that matched no queue and were dropped.

Section 4: Limits (how close is RabbitMQ to blocking?)

Memory vs RabbitMQ limit: % of RabbitMQ's memory limit used.
Disk space used: how full each data disk is.
Connection and process limits: % of connection handles and internal processes used.
Alert rules

Pod alerts

#	Alert	Fires when	Severity
1	RabbitMQPodNotReady	A pod is not ready for 5 min	Critical
2	RabbitMQPodRestartingFrequently	A pod restarts more than 3 times in 15 min	Warning
3	RabbitMQReplicasMismatch	Fewer pods are running than expected for 10 min	Warning
4	RabbitMQClusterQuorumLost	Fewer than 2 of 3 pods are ready (cluster can't work)	Critical
5	RabbitMQMetricsTargetDown	Prometheus can't reach a pod for 5 min	Critical
6	RabbitMQMetricsAbsent	No RabbitMQ data at all for 10 min	Critical

Resource alerts

#	Alert	Fires when	Severity
7	RabbitMQMemoryAlarm	RabbitMQ hit its memory limit and is blocking messages	Critical
8	RabbitMQDiskAlarm	Disk is too low and RabbitMQ is blocking messages	Critical
9	RabbitMQFileDescriptorAlarm	Too many connections, so new ones are refused	Critical
10	RabbitMQHighMemoryUsage	Memory is above 85% of the limit for 10 min	Warning
11	RabbitMQLowDiskHeadroom	Free disk is getting close to the blocking limit	Warning
12	RabbitMQPVCAlmostFull	Data disk is more than 80% full	Warning
13	RabbitMQHighFileDescriptorUsage	Connection handles are more than 80% used	Warning
14	RabbitMQHighCPUUsage	CPU is above 85% of the limit for 15 min	Warning

Queue alerts (all queues combined)

#	Alert	Fires when	Severity
15	RabbitMQQueueBacklogHigh	More than 1,000 messages waiting for 10 min	Warning
16	RabbitMQQueueBacklogCritical	More than 10,000 messages waiting for 10 min	Critical
17	RabbitMQNoConsumers	Messages are waiting but no app is reading them	Warning
18	RabbitMQUnackedMessagesHigh	More than 1,000 messages picked up but not finished	Warning
19	RabbitMQUnroutableMessages	Messages are being dropped because they matched no queue	Warning

In short: 8 alerts are critical, meaning RabbitMQ is down or blocking messages, so act now. The other 11 are warnings, meaning something is heading toward a problem, so check it soon.
